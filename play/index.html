Race the champion

Can you beat a harness that taught itself to drive?

Cornfield Crossing is one of the four tracks the system never trained on. The cyan kart is our champion after 40 rounds of self-improvement, frozen: it drives exactly the lap it recorded, 56.9 s. The small window shows that lap in the game and the answers its driver gave.

Your kart has the game kart's top speed (25 m/s), acceleration and turning circle; no drifting, no nitro. Leave the road and you slow down. Reach the finish line before the champion does.
