Race our AI

Can you beat an AI that taught itself to drive?

The red kart is our AI: the best version of its driving program after 40 rounds of rewriting it, now frozen. Cornfield Crossing is one of the tracks it never practiced on. It drives exactly the lap it recorded there, 56.9 s; the small window shows that lap in the game and the answers its driver gave.

You drive the blue kart: the same kart on the same track, with the game kart's top speed (25 m/s), acceleration and turning circle; no drifting, no nitro. Leave the road and you slow down. Reach the finish line before the red kart does.
